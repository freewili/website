Humpback Orca

HumpbackOrca
CAN-Only Connectivity with Optional DC V+ Power for FREE-WILi 2

The Humpback Orca is the CAN-only version of SAN DIEGO Orca. It retains the power supply and routes FREE-WILi 2's existing internal ISO CAN FD interface to a rugged DB-15 connector. The RS485, 10BASE-T1L, 10BASE-T1S, and digital I/O circuitry are omitted.

This is the host's existing CAN channel. Humpback adds no CAN controller, transceiver, or CAN termination. Plug it into the FREE-WILi 2 extension connector to access CAN through the DB-15 for logging, scripting, and testing vehicle, robot, and industrial networks.

The DC V+ input is optional and is only needed if you want to power and charge FREE-WILi 2 from an external 5–60 V DC supply.* You can use CAN without connecting DC V+.

CAN and power

ISO CAN FD
FREE-WILi 2's internal CAN channel routed to DB-15 pins 6 (CAN H) and 14 (CAN L); no additional CAN channel
Termination
Humpback routes the host's existing CAN bus and adds no CAN termination components
DC V+ Input
Optional 5–60 V DC input to power and charge FREE-WILi 2; positive on pin 15, with pin 8 as return*
Connector
Rugged DB-15 for clean, secure harness connections in lab, garage, or field

Power input specifications

Humpback retains the SAN DIEGO Orca power supply. Connect DC V+ only when you want to power and charge FREE-WILi 2 from an external DC supply; CAN operation does not require this connection.

Input voltage
5–60 V DC*
Wiring / polarity
Supply positive to DB-15 pin 15 (DC V+); supply negative to pin 8 (GND)
Internal supply
The on-board buck converter feeds the host's 5 V rail through the extension connector. Internal supply rating: 5 V at 2 A (10 W)
Supply sizing
Input current depends on input voltage, load, and conversion losses. The 2 A figure refers to the internal 5 V supply, not a fixed DC V+ input current. Allow voltage headroom above 5 V for a regulated 5 V rail under load
Power path
The internal 5 V output includes ideal-diode reverse-current blocking. This is not reverse-polarity protection at the DC V+ input; observe the input polarity
Ground reference
Pin 8 is the shared signal and power return, connected to host ground. The power input is not galvanically isolated

* DEF CON FREE-WILi 2 edition: the DC V+ power input does not function.

DB-15 pinout

The view below looks straight into the module's male DB-15 pins with the label facing up. The wide row of eight is on top, with pin 1 at the top left. Only CAN and power connections are used on Humpback.

Pins marked NC in the diagram are unused on Humpback. Its CAN and power connections use the same pin assignments as SAN DIEGO Orca.

CAN with FREE-WILi 2

Humpback brings FREE-WILi 2's internal CAN interface to a DB-15 harness connection. Use it with the host's touchscreen, onboard Linux, Python scripting, and wireless connectivity.

Log bus traffic to a script, display live CAN data on-screen, bridge frames over WiFi, or run a full automated test sequence from your pocket.

Choose SAN DIEGO Orca when you also need RS485, 10BASE-T1L, 10BASE-T1S, or digital I/O.
