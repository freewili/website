Jambu Serial LED Control Orca

JambuOrca
8-Channel LED Driver Breakout for FREE-WILi

The Jambu Serial LED Control Orca is a compact and powerful 8-channel LED driver breakout module designed to bring dynamic lighting to your projects. Whether you’re driving LED strips, individual LEDs, or incandescent bulbs, Jambu gives you full serial control over every channel through a simple two-wire I²C interface.

Powered via a 5V barrel jack and compatible with common anode Serial RGB LED strips, Jambu integrates seamlessly with FREE-WILi and any QWIIC-equipped system — making it a versatile building block for lighting installations, interactive displays, and hardware prototypes.
