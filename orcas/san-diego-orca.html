SAN DIEGO Orca

SANDIEGOOrca
RS485, CAN FD, 10BASE-T1S/T1L Automotive and Industrial Interface

The SAN DIEGO Orca snaps onto your FREE-WILi 2 and turns the open electronics multitool into a full vehicle and industrial network interface. You get ISO CAN FD, RS485, and both flavors of single-pair automotive Ethernet (10BASE-T1S and 10BASE-T1L) through one rugged DB-15 connector, powered straight from the vehicle battery via VBATT.

Modern vehicles and industrial systems do not speak USB. They speak CAN FD, RS485, and increasingly, single-pair Ethernet. SAN DIEGO Orca brings all of them to FREE-WILi 2 so you can sniff, script, simulate, and stress-test the same buses running in cars, trucks, robots, and factory floors.

Plug it into the FREE-WILi extension connector, wire up the DB-15, and you are on the network with the same Python, Rust, C/C++ API, touchscreen, and onboard Linux workflow you already use.

What is on board

ISO CAN FD
1x channel with selectable on-board termination for end-node or tap use
RS485
1x channel for Modbus, motor drives, sensor chains, and legacy field systems
10BASE-T1S
1x multidrop single-pair Ethernet for modern zonal vehicle networks
10BASE-T1L
1x long-reach single-pair Ethernet up to 1 km for industrial/building automation
VBATT Input
1x vehicle-battery input to power the setup directly from source
Connector
All I/O routes through one rugged DB-15 harness interface

Automotive Teams

Industrial and IoT
Developers bridging RS485 field devices and 10BASE-T1L sensor networks
Makers and Students
Learners building real automotive and industrial protocol skills on open hardware

Why FREE-WILi + SAN DIEGO

A USB-CAN adapter does CAN. A USB-RS485 stick does RS485. A T1S media converter does T1S. SAN DIEGO Orca does all of it on one module attached to a device with a touchscreen, dual RP2350s, an FPGA, onboard Linux, Python scripting, and wireless connectivity.

Log CAN traffic to scripts, bridge it over WiFi, display live bus data on-screen, and automate full test sequences without carrying a laptop.
