Get it

On device

  1. Download the FW2 appstore and flash it to your device's display CPU
  2. Select the app from the list.
  3. Press the download button to save the app to the device's RAM and execute it.

Device clients fetch ../api/v1/catalog.json and the artifact file paths directly.
