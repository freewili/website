FREE-WILi 2 upgrades to a 3.5″ 480×320 capacitive touchscreen — so much more is possible on this screen compared to its predecessor. The panel is driven by its own dedicated display RP2350, keeping rendering workload completely separate from the main application processor.

Combined with the 14-button layout, the larger capacitive touch panel delivers dramatically better input than anything FreeWili1 offered — a meaningful step up for both interactive tools and gaming.
